Our Approach

A structured, science-driven methodology to deliver reliable results at every project stage

01

Data Integration

Systematic compilation and cross-validation of all available geological, geophysical, geochemical and production data.

02

Conceptual Model

Construction of a coherent subsurface model capturing the key controls on fluid flow, heat transfer and pressure.

03

Numerical Simulation

Translation of the conceptual model into a calibrated numerical simulator, matched against historical field behaviour.

04

Scenario Analysis

Systematic exploration of parameter uncertainty and operational scenarios to bound resource potential and risk.

05

Forecast & Recommendation

Delivery of production forecasts, optimised strategies and clear, actionable recommendations for field operators.
